## 1. Scope & Definitions

**"Portfolio"** means debtor account data (borrower details, loan amounts, dues, contact information) uploaded by Partner to the SamvadX platform.

**"Data"** includes all Portfolio content and any derivative data (call logs, agent notes, payment records) generated during collection activities.

**"Platform"** means the SamvadX web application and all associated services, APIs, and infrastructure operated by Axiotta.

**Partner retains full ownership** of Portfolio data and remains solely responsible for:
- **Accuracy:** All borrower information, loan amounts, and contact details
- **Legality:** Ensuring compliance with all debt collection laws (TDSAT, RBI guidelines, NCRB)
- **Consent:** Obtaining borrower consent for collection activities where required
- **Confidentiality:** Protecting borrower PII and not disclosing it outside the Portal

**Axiotta is not liable** for:
- Inaccurate or false data in Portfolio (e.g., wrong phone numbers, outdated addresses)
- Legal violations arising from how Partner uses the data
- Regulatory fines or borrower complaints stemming from Partner's collection practices

## 4. Data Security & Privacy

**Axiotta will:**
- Encrypt all data in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- Implement role-based access controls (agents see only assigned accounts)
- Maintain audit logs of all data access and modifications
- Comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023 and applicable RBI regulations
- Notify Partner within 24 hours of any confirmed data breach

**Partner will:**
- Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication
- Not share login credentials with unauthorized personnel
- Report suspected breaches or misuse immediately to support@samvadx.com
